# Deferred scientific ideas

## Internal-background normalization — deferred

Explore sufficiently abundant internal RNA 5′ positions as empirical controls
when enzyme-dependent composition shifts undermine global scaling assumptions.
For bacterial and archaeal experiments, optional GFF/CDS annotations could help
identify and stratify candidate internal positions; annotation membership or
abundance alone would not establish invariance.

A future scientific design should define abundance/stability criteria, treatment-
independent selection or held-out validation, coordinate/strand conventions,
annotation provenance and safeguards against selecting genuine enzyme-responsive
sites. Compare with explicit monophosphate controls and existing normalization
on independently approved datasets. No method, selector, genome mapping or new
normalization algorithm is implemented or authorized by this backlog entry.

## Independent R-checker warnings

Retain the previously observed namespace-load stack-imbalance warnings from the
independent R checker as an explicit follow-up. Production normalization and the
independent checker are distinct paths; successful numerical checks do not explain
the warning's native cause. Diagnose only in a separately approved task. Do not
repair packages or change normalization algorithms as part of documentation finalization.
